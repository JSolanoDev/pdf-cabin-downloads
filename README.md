Installers are byte-for-byte copies of the verified release builds. Windows
builds remain unsigned previews; macOS builds are not notarized. Keep original
documents and inspect saved results. PDF/font restrictions and device limits
apply. The Android APK is not a Google Play listing.

PDF processing stays on your device. Downloads are delivered by GitHub's public
release infrastructure and are subject to its privacy policies. Support issues
are public; never attach private PDFs, credentials or personal information.

The software remains MIT licensed; third-party components retain their licenses
and bundled notices. Private repository visibility does not change those terms.

in development; see the app's legal and OCR notices for details.
